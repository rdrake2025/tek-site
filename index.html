        Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Write the seed phrase on paper and never type it into a website.

      2. Get some SOL

        Buy SOL on an exchange and send it to your wallet address.

        Only from this page or the pinned post on TEK's X account. Look-alike coins will copy the name.
