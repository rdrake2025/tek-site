What this checks

  • Mint and freeze authority

    Whether anyone can still print more of the coin, or freeze it in your wallet.

  • Who holds it

    The biggest wallets, how much of the supply they sit on, and whether the creator still holds a pile.

  • Who made it

    How many other coins the creator has launched, how many in the last day, and what the best of them is worth now. Some wallets launch fifty a day.

  • The pool

    How much liquidity is actually behind it, and whether the pool tokens are locked or sitting in someone's wallet.

  • Volume against liquidity

    Volume many times deeper than the pool usually means the same wallets trading with each other.

  • Age

    A coin minutes old has almost nothing to analyse, and this page says so instead of calling it clean.

What it can't see

Whether a dev will sell tomorrow. Whether the community is real people or bots. Whether a pile of wallets belongs to one person, unless it is obvious on-chain. A quiet result is not a safe result, and none of this is financial advice.
